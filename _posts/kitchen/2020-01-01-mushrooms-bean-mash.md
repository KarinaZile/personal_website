**Ingredients:**

* oyster mushrooms
* Brown Shimeji mushrooms
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 30g olive oil
* 1 red onion
* 1 red Romano pepper
* 50g garlic (about 10 cloves)
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 30g tomato paste
* 3 teaspoons Chipotle powder
* 3 teaspoons cumin powder
* 1 teaspoons coriander powder
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 700g white pre-cooked beans (cannellini, white kidney beans, butter beans, etc.)
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
* 40g lemon juice

**Methods:**

* Preheat a large pan with olive oil. Chop the mushrooms and fry them until golden.
* Chop the onion and the pepper into large chunks, peel the garlic cloves and chop big ones in half. Place all of these into a small pan with 30g of olive oil. Fry gently until garlic becomes golden on the outside.
* Meanwhile, prepare a small bowl with tomato paste, spices (Chipotle, cumin and coriander powder) and salt.
* Warm up the beans in the microwave. Add salt and 40g of lemon juice. Handblender into a mash. Taste and adjust salt and acidity (lemon juice).
* Once the garlic is looking ready, take the small pan off the heat. Add the contents of the tomato paste bowl and a tiny bit of water. Mix everything together, turn the heat back on and stir until the tomato paste develops a rust colour. Take off the heat, let it cool down for a bit, then handblender it into a paste.
* Mix the paste with the fried mushrooms. Taste and adjust salt and spiciness (chilli powder).
* Serve bean mash topped with spicy mushrooms.

**Inspired by:** [Flavour](https://www.penguin.co.uk/books/111/1116203/ottolenghi-flavour/9781785038938.html) cookbook by Yotam Ottolenghi.
