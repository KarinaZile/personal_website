**Ingredients for 2 portions:**

* olive oil
* 1 red onion
* 30g garlic (about 6 cloves)
* 30g tomato paste
* 3 teaspoons Berbere spice mix
* 0.5 teaspoon salt
* 400g of tomatoes (or a tomato can)
* 400g pre-cooked black beans
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 2 plantains
* 1 lime

**Methods:**
* Finely dice the onion. Separately, finely dice your tomatoes.
* Pre-heat a Dutch oven with a splash of oil. Fry the onion until it is just starting to brown.
* Add the garlic through the garlic press. Add tomato paste, Berbere spice mix and salt. Stir until the tomato paste develops a rust colour, then add tomatoes.
* Add (defrosted) black beans and simmer gently.
* Preheat a frying pan with a splash of olive oil.
* Cut the plantains in half, then half them lengthwise. Fry flat side down until the flat sides become nicely browned, then cover with a lid and reduce heat to allow them to cook through and develop sweetness. 
* Once the plantains are ready, de-oil them with a paper towel. Sprinkle them with lime juice. Serve with the beans.
