**Ingredients for 2 portions:**

* 1-2 beetroots
* olive oil
* hazelnuts
* 180g puy lentils
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 1 red onion
* 3 cloves of garlic (about 15g)
* 3 sundried tomatoes in oil (about 30g)
* 30g tomato paste
* 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
* 0.5 teaspoon Kashmiri chilli powder
* 0.25 teaspoon salt
* 400g can of tomatoes
* 10g tamari soy sauce
<nbsp></nbsp>
* 15g shaoxing rice vinegar
* strong mature goat cheese
* delicate cruciferous greens (watercress, rocket, etc.)

**Methods:**
* Preheat the oven to 200C.
* Peel the beetroots and cut them into bite-size cubes. Pile them up onto a baking tray lined with baking paper, add a splash of olive oil to coat them, spread them out into a single layer and bake until golden brown.
* Start boiling your lentils.
* Place the hazelnuts onto a different tray and toast them in the oven for about 5 minutes.
* Open the tomato can. If they are not already finely chopped, handblender them.
* Once there is no more danger of burning the hazelnuts, dice the onion, crush the garlic through a garlic press, finely chop the sundried tomatoes.
* Pre-heat a generous splash of olive oil in a Dutch oven. Add in the onion and saute them for a bit on medium heat, stirring occasionally. 
* Add in garlic, sundried tomatoes, tomato paste, the spice mix (smoked paprika, chilli powder, salt). Stir until the tomato paste develops a rust colour. Whenever things start sticking to the bottom of the pot, add a splash of water.
* Add in the tomato can and tamari soy sauce. 
* Once the lentils are getting tender, add them in.
* Crumble the goat cheese and chop the hazelnuts roughly into halves.
* Mix the vinegar into the lentil. Serve topped with beetroots, goat cheese, hazelnuts and cruciferous greens.
