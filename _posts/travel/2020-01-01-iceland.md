### Reasons to go include:

##### 1. F208
* Beautiful mossy lava fields around (63.917734, -18.602217).
* Beautiful area around (63.939573, -18.641901). From here you could do a short hike to Gígvatn (63.93724, -18.663614).
* F223 is a short detour to a beautiful view (63.95137, -18.638244). From here you could hike to Ófaerufoss (63.964216, -18.617319).
* F235 is a big detour to Langisjór lake. Great view of Hellnafjall (64.069302, -18.499554). Great view of Langisjór lake (64.113381, -18.432485). See [Igor's blog](https://epiciceland.net/langisjor-guide/#Langisjor_hiking_trails) for hiking suggestions.
* If you are driving north on F208, the most magnificent view of the whole drive is at (63.981984, -18.767118) where you enter a giant crater that contains a world of its own. On your way south, the best view is at (63.979225, -18.890101) when you enter the crater from the other side.
* Great view of Kýlingavatn at (63.988466, -18.992473).
* From this parking (64.013316, -19.04276), climb up the little Stútur volcano (64.013136, -19.040676) for great views. You could continue hiking to Norðurnámur peak (64.020365, -19.015206).
* From this parking (64.013784, -19.045826), walk to this viewpoint (64.01202, -19.045744) where you can see the new lava that entered Frostastaðavatn in the old crater.
* View of Ljótipollur crater from this parking (64.034714, -19.013739).
* View of Hnausapollur crater from this parking (64.054984, -19.03453).
* Continuing along F225 is on my wishlist.
* Also on my wishlist is to drive the loop from the east end of F210 (63.734554, -18.627465) to the south end of F232 (63.630994, -18.515209). Expected highlights include Axlafoss (63.806557, -18.764642), hiking from this parking (63.830287, -18.812118) to Rauðibotn crater (63.840471, -18.838185), driving over Bláfjallafoss (63.759345, -18.840717), Hólmsárfoss (63.710231, -18.720101). If you've seen videos of cars driving over a waterfall, that's Bláfjallafoss.



##### 2. F210 and F261 via Maelifell
* The part of F210 near Maelifell was closed when we visited, so we ended up doing a loop from F210 west (63.824869, -20.028807) to F261 west (63.712784, -19.736916), and it was one of our favourite drives. 
* This pedestrian bridge (63.825508, -19.213682) is really fun.
* This is the point (63.800302, -18.955723) where the road closure is likely, luckily you get a perfect view of Maelifell from here. 
* Hattafell (63.791284, -19.353733) is not as famous as Maelifell, but I'd say it is equally as pretty. 
* Traditional turf houses here (63.783698, -19.427065).
* From the numerous waterfalls along the way, this one (63.777344, -19.417508) was my favourite. 
* The best view of magnificent Einhyrningur (63.740033, -19.472699) is from (63.745128, -19.465478).
* On my wishlist is driving F249 to Thórsmörk (63.677721, -19.481827) and hiking from there, perhaps to Fimmvörðuháls mountain pass (63.621547, -19.440714). See [Igor's blog](https://epiciceland.net/detailed-guide-thorsmork-iceland/). If you've seen videos of buses barely surviving a river crossing, that's Krossá river around here (63.684329, -19.512294). 



##### 3. F206 to Laki craters 
* Fagrifoss (63.868037, -18.244087) is beautiful, totally worth the 250m walk from this parking (63.869909, -18.243945).
* Once you reach the loop (63.981026, -18.285304), it is implicitly assumed that you'll drive it conter clockwise (turn right).
* Hike up Mt Laki for the best views. From this parking (64.062678, -18.244497), do a Visitor trail first - through (64.067108, -18.247407) and up to this viewpoint (64.067734, -18.245519). Then continue to this viewpoint (64.068514, -18.242177) and summit Mt Laki (64.070189, -18.236308). If you see buses at the bus parking lot (64.065146, -18.246338), do this hike in reverse. 
* Tjarnargígur crater hike from this parking (64.041539, -18.312448). Highlights include the crater lake (64.039331, -18.305363) and entering this crater (64.034941, -18.316457). It is a circular hike that comes back to the parking lot, so won't have to walk along the road.
* This is a great viewpoint (64.073858, -18.262858) you can drive to. 
* This is a tiny crater (63.995154, -18.408037) you can peak into.
* On my wishlist is hiking from (64.056433, -18.294544). There is a hike that goes via this peak (64.081896, -18.338716), along this ridge (64.049215, -18.426328), to this waterfall (64.018034, -18.456555). For more information, speak to the ranger at (64.062678, -18.244497).
* Check online for tours to Laki craters, see what time they are likely to be there (10:30?) and avoid that time.



##### 4. F910 to Askja
* For a great viewpoint of Iceland desert, park at (65.345111, -15.770673) and hike up to (65.353587, -15.765903).
* For another great viewpoint, park at (65.143034, -15.946029) and hike up Dyngja (65.154234, -15.942384).
* The views on F910 become spectacular from (65.093969, -16.162647) onwards. 
* You'll have to unlock the gate of the Vatnajökull National Park at (65.099701, -16.172708) and then another one further down the road. If the gates are not padlocked, you are expected to open and close them to let yourself in.
* The hike to Askja starts from this carpark (65.067057, -16.724943) at the end of the road. Explore the craters around (65.065121, -16.73083). When you reach the Víti crater (65.047875, -16.724685), go right for this viewpoint (65.046184, -16.727618) and come back. Then continue all the way down to the shore of Askja lake (65.045057, -16.725202). The ranger suggested we return to the carpark via (65.047708, -16.722528) and (65.048684, -16.70817) for extra good views.
* If you have time, explore the Drekagil canyon. From here (65.042834, -16.596094) you can hike all the way to Drekafoss (65.041997, -16.60667).
* On my wishlist is continuing on F910 past Askja to (64.933184, -16.514652) and (64.921269, -16.721375) and beyond.
* Also on my wishlist is exploring F902 to (64.725227, -16.653609) and F903; including Hvannalindir oasis (64.888703, -16.308538).
* See [Igor's blog](https://epiciceland.net/guide-how-to-visit-askja/). Both park rangers and our hotel staff said they don't like F88.



##### 5. Hiking in Fjallabak Nature Reserve
* Once you pass the parking camera at (64.001847, -19.045635), you'll have to pay for parking. This parking lot (63.995027, -19.059698) can be reached without river crossing via Road 26 (64.209511, -19.152276), it fills up fast. If you are able to cross rivers, continue to the parking area further down the road. During peak season, you might have to reserve your parking in advance, check [this website](https://ust.is/english/visiting-iceland/protected-areas/south/fjallabak-nature-reserve/landmannalaugar/).
* I did a circular hike from Landmannalaugar camp via Brennisteinsalda and Bláhnúkur peaks. I strongly advise you do it anti-clockwise. The hike starts at (63.990613, -19.061659) and takes you through a lava field to (63.990079, -19.085887). Great viewpoint here (63.989264, -19.089848). This bit (63.989846, -19.103054) is very steep and a bit tricky, I would not want to do it on the way down. The best viewpoint in the area is from Brennisteinsalda (63.980905, -19.095541), it is not to be missed. Some geothermal activity here (63.980398, -19.09108) and more vigorous geothermal activity here (63.981819, -19.088425). From here you have 3 options: hiking up Bláhnúkur, walking around it to (63.986232, -19.060777) or returning through the lava field (63.990079, -19.085887); the trail that used to go through (63.983242, -19.085833) no longer exists. If you head up Bláhnúkur, there is a river crossing here (63.978858, -19.083731) - it was only a trickle when I was there, but sometimes it is serious. This bit (63.977131, -19.083097) is steep and very muddy, I would not want to do it on the way down. From Bláhnúkur (63.976775, -19.069172) you get an amazing 360 view. On your way to the carpark, you'll pass this viewpoint (63.986232, -19.060777) of an unbelievably blue mountain. 
* There is more amazing hiking in the area. On my wishlist is a circular hike via Skalli peak (63.95305, -19.0428), and a Graenihryggur ridge hike from (63.985068, -18.967707) to the blue mountain viewpoint (63.936188, -19.005633) and back.
* See [Igor's blog](https://epiciceland.net/landmannalaugar-1-day-hike-guide/).



##### 6. Inside the Volcano
* This is the only evelator into a volcano crater in the world. Book in advance. 
* Take Road 417 from (64.064698, -21.579127) to the meeting point at (63.982785, -21.652324). The tour starts with a 3km walk to the base camp (63.997664, -21.698592). From near there you'll take an elevator into Thríhnúkagígur crater. As you enter the elevator, left side is best for the views of a vertical lava tube. You'll have about 20 minutes inside the crater - just enought to explore the two short hiking trails inside.

Summer in Iceland is the best time to see puffins. From May through mid-August, puffins are out in full force. This is when they nest along the cliffs, feed their chicks, and fly back and forth with fish tucked into their colorful beaks. It is the perfect season to visit places like Latrabjarg Cliffs, the Westman Islands, or Borgarfjordur Eystri, where large puffin colonies are easy to spot up close.
